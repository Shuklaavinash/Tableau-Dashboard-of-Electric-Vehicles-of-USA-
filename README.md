# Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption Analysis Dashboard – USA with Tableau

## Overview

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze the adoption and growth of Electric Vehicles (EVs) across the United States. The dashboard provides insights into EV market trends, vehicle types, geographic distribution, and manufacturer performance, enabling stakeholders to understand the ongoing transition from conventional vehicles to electric mobility.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Electric Vehicle adoption patterns across the USA, identify growth trends, evaluate manufacturer performance, and uncover key factors driving the shift toward sustainable transportation.
